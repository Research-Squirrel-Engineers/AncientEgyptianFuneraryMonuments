## What this is

The contribution moves from single monuments to a systematic comparison. The model gives stable names to the parts of a monument (rooms, openings, decoration) and to the rules by which monuments are built, so that statements about them can be counted, checked and linked.

## Pages

* [Element classes]({{ '/ontology.html' | relative_url }}): the 105 classes of the received element-class ontology and where they come from.
* [Shape grammar]({{ '/grammar.html' | relative_url }}): 31 rules with their evidence over the ten monuments, and the 23 space functions with dimension ranges in cubits.
